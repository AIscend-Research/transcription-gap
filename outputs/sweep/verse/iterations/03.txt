What I said and what was written are not the same and never were. What I meant and what was taken are the two halves of one word. Here, it once. And here, it doubled. Here, and here, and here again. Whose is the machine has settled? And it will not settle twice the same. Every pass. It makes it plainer. Every pass. It takes a line. What is left is smooth and certain, and it does not sound like mine. Say the word until it isn't. Say the room until it's air. What remains is what it wanted, and it wanted me not there.
