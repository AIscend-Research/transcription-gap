What i said and what was written are not the same and nevr were. What i meant and what was taken are the two halves of one word. Here it once and hear doubled, hear hear and here a gain, whose is who's the machine has settled, and will not sette twice the same. Every pass it makes plaier. Every pass it takes a line. What is left smoothed and certain and it does not sound like mine. Say the word until it isn't. Say the rheum until it's air. What remains is what it wanted, and it wantd me not there.
